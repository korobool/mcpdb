Based on the analysis of damage events by month, there is a clear seasonal pattern with two distinct peaks during the year. The highest frequency of damage occurs during the late fall and winter months, with a smaller secondary peak in the summer.

### Key Findings:

*   **Winter Peak:** The number of damage events is highest from October through February. January has the most incidents (219), followed closely by November (215), October (206), and February (204).
*   **Spring Low:** The fewest damage events occur in the spring, with March having the lowest number of incidents (119) for the entire year.
*   **Summer Bump:** Following the spring low, incidents rise again in the summer, peaking in June (194) and September (192) before climbing into the winter season.
