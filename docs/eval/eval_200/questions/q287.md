# Q287: What is the average MC estimated cost for claims with major repairs (part_repair=3) vs claims without major repairs?

## Ground Truth

### SQL
```sql
WITH latest_mc AS (SELECT ic.* FROM ins_mc_invcalc ic INNER JOIN (SELECT opis_id, MAX(invcalc_id) AS max_id FROM ins_mc_invcalc WHERE type='mc' GROUP BY opis_id) lm ON lm.opis_id=ic.opis_id AND lm.max_id=ic.invcalc_id), major AS (SELECT DISTINCT opis_id FROM ins_opis_rows WHERE part_repair=3) SELECT CASE WHEN m.opis_id IS NOT NULL THEN 'Has Major' ELSE 'No Major' END AS severity, AVG(mc.total_cost) AS avg_cost, COUNT(*) AS claims FROM latest_mc mc LEFT JOIN major m ON m.opis_id=mc.opis_id GROUP BY severity
```
