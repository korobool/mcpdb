## Agent Response

Of the parts analyzed, the vast majority originate from the SmartClaiMS catalog, with a much smaller portion coming from free text entries.

Here is the specific distribution of the 6,648 `opis_rows` based on their `part_id`:

*   **SmartClaiMS (part_id < 10M):** 5,902 rows
*   **Free Text (part_id >= 20M):** 746 rows

### Key Findings

*   **Dominance of SmartClaiMS:** Structured parts from the SmartClaiMS catalog represent approximately **89%** of the total rows.
*   **Minority of Free Text:** Free text parts, which are not tied to a standardized catalog, make up the remaining **11%**. This indicates that most parts are selected from a predefined, structured source rather than being manually entered.
