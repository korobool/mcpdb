# Q297: For claims with both repairs and MC estimates, what is the average MC cost vs damage total cost?

## Ground Truth

### SQL
```sql
WITH latest_mc AS (SELECT ic.* FROM ins_mc_invcalc ic INNER JOIN (SELECT opis_id, MAX(invcalc_id) AS max_id FROM ins_mc_invcalc WHERE type='mc' GROUP BY opis_id) lm ON lm.opis_id=ic.opis_id AND lm.max_id=ic.invcalc_id) SELECT AVG(mc.total_cost) AS avg_mc_cost, AVG(d.total_cost) AS avg_damage_cost, COUNT(*) AS claims FROM latest_mc mc JOIN ins_opis o ON o.opis_id=mc.opis_id JOIN ins_damages d ON d.damage_id=o.damage_id INNER JOIN ins_invrepair ir ON ir.opis_id=mc.opis_id
```

## Agent Execution

### Step 1 (SQL) — OK
```sql
SELECT
  AVG(T3.total_cost) AS average_mc_cost,
  AVG(T1.total_cost) AS average_damage_total_cost
FROM ins_damages AS T1
INNER JOIN ins_opis AS T2
  ON T1.damage_id = T2.damage_id
INNER JOIN ins_mc_invcalc AS T3
  ON T2.opis_id = T3.opis_id
INNER JOIN ins_invrepair AS T4
  ON T2.opis_id = T4.opis_id
WHERE
  T3.is_active = 1;
```

### Step 2 (SQL) — OK
```sql
SELECT
  AVG(T3.total_cost) AS average_mc_cost,
  AVG(T1.total_cost) AS average_damage_total_cost
FROM ins_damages AS T1
INNER JOIN ins_opis AS T2
  ON T1.damage_id = T2.damage_id
INNER JOIN ins_mc_invcalc AS T3
  ON T2.opis_id = T3.opis_id
INNER JOIN ins_invrepair AS T4
  ON T2.opis_id = T4.opis_id
WHERE
  T3.is_active = 1;
```

For claims that include both repair records and MC estimates, the average MC cost is **$879.49**, while the average damage total cost is **$63.71**.

### Key Finding

The most significant finding is the stark difference between the two figures. The **average Managed Care cost is more than 13 times greater than the average damage total cost** for this specific subset of claims. This suggests that when a claim involves both property damage and managed care components, the costs associated with the managed care portion are substantially higher than the direct physical damage costs.
