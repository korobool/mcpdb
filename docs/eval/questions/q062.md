## Ground Truth

**SQL:**
```sql
SELECT AVG(YEAR(d.damage_date) - c.car_year) FROM ins_opis o JOIN ins_damages d ON o.damage_id = d.damage_id JOIN ins_cars c ON o.car_id = c.car_id WHERE d.damage_date IS NOT NULL AND c.car_year > 0 AND YEAR(d.damage_date) >= c.car_year
```

## Agent Response

Based on the analysis, the average age of a car at the time it was damaged is approximately **18.21 years**.

This finding indicates that, on average, vehicles are over 18 years old when they are involved in a reported damage incident. This was calculated by finding the difference between the year the damage occurred and the car's manufacturing year for all incidents and then averaging those ages.
