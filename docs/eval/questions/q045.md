### Key Findings:
*   **Bumper Damage is Most Common:** Three of the top five most frequently damaged parts are components of the front or rear bumpers (Front bumper lining, Rear bumper lining, and Front bumper). This strongly suggests that low-speed, front and rear-end collisions are the most common type of incident.
*   **Front-End Dominance:** The front hood and two front bumper components are in the top five, highlighting the high frequency of damage to the front of vehicles.
