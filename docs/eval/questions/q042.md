## Agent Response

Based on the analysis, after normalizing variations in location names, the three most common damage places are:

1.  **София** (Sofia)
2.  **Пловдив** (Plovdiv)
3.  **Варна** (Varna)

### Key Findings

*   **Sofia is Overwhelmingly the Most Common Location**: Sofia is the most frequent damage place by a significant margin, with **1,206** recorded incidents.
*   **Data Normalization was Crucial**: An initial analysis showed multiple variations for Sofia (e.g., `София`, `гр. София`, `България, София, Околовръстен път`). After consolidating these into a single "София" category, its true frequency became clear.
*   **Distant Second and Third**: The next two most common cities, Plovdiv and Varna, have substantially fewer incidents, with just **11** and **9** respectively.

### Step 1 (sql) — success (19ms)

```sql
WITH cleaned_places AS (
  SELECT
    CASE
      WHEN damage_place LIKE '%София%' THEN 'София'
      ELSE TRIM(REPLACE(damage_place, 'гр. ', ''))
    END AS cleaned_damage_place
  FROM ins_damages
  WHERE damage_place IS NOT NULL AND damage_place != ''
)
SELECT
  cleaned_damage_place AS damage_place,
  COUNT(*) AS frequency
FROM cleaned_places
GROUP BY
  cleaned_damage_place
ORDER BY
  frequency DESC
LIMIT 3;
```
